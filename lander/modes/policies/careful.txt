Safety first. Above 30 m, fall no faster than 3 m/s; below 30 m, no faster than 1.5 m/s. While the pad is still more than 20 m to the side, do not descend below 40 m: hold height and move toward the pad. Move sideways only slowly and stop drifting once over the pad. Touch down very gently and upright. Use fuel freely if it keeps the ship safe.
